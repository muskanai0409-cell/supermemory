## 3. Eon in plain words

**What it does.** Eon backs up a company's cloud systems on AWS, Azure and Google Cloud. It finds every resource on its own (servers, databases, storage, data warehouses), works out what each one holds, and applies backup policies automatically. Eon calls this "Cloud Backup Posture Management". The backups are stored in open, compressed formats (Parquet and Iceberg). That means the backup copy can be searched, queried with SQL or plain English, and reused as a data lake for analytics and AI. Eon claims storage savings of up to 50%. ([Enterprise Times](https://www.enterprisetimes.co.uk/2024/10/01/eon-emerges-from-stealth-with-127-million-funding/), [Blocks & Files](https://blocksandfiles.com/2026/01/09/eon-turns-cloud-backups-into-sql-searchable-parquet-data-lakes/), [eon.io/platform](https://www.eon.io/platform)) [High]

- **How it describes itself in 2026:** "the Autonomous Data Foundation for the AI era". Products: Data Protection, CBPM, Live Data Lake, Eon AI Agent, and Eon Mind, which makes company data usable as context for Claude, Gemini, Databricks and Snowflake through MCP ([eon.io/eon-mind](https://www.eon.io/eon-mind)). [Med]
- **Named customers:** SoFi, NETGEAR, AlphaSense, Innago, StructuredWeb and Rise ([eon.io/customers](https://www.eon.io/customers)). [Med] Eon has never published ARR. In December 2025 Ehrlich told Reuters that revenue had "more than tripled" over the year and that Eon has "a few tens" of large customers ([Yahoo/Reuters](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/cloud-data-startup-eons-valuation-140342422.html)). [High]
- **Offices:** New York and Tel Aviv. [High]

**Era, by Eon (new, 4 Oct 2026).** These details come straight from the [repo README](https://github.com/eon-io/era). [High]
- Era generates a complete synthetic company on demand. It serves the data in each vendor's real API format and over MCP.
- README count: "61 systems and counting". The research papers say 66 product simulators [Med].
- "100% synthetic … free for builders". Web console: [console.era.eon.io](https://console.era.eon.io/).
- **Brand colours from the repo:** orange `#FF5C00`, near-black `#1C1512`, pink `#F2A9B4`, taupe `#8A7A6D`, cream `#FDF6EC`. Logo and wordmark SVGs are in the repo's `assets/` folder.
- **Research numbers, all from snippets of arXiv abstracts, so verify before use** [Med/unverified]:
  - Nine AI models scored between 42.4% and 76.8% on one generated company.
  - On average they got 92.6% on simple filtering questions but only 3.7% on multi-hop questions.
  - On the hidden-knowledge questions, the agents got 1 right out of 84 attempts in total.
  - Across 23 generated companies, average realism rose from 61.8 to 97.0, with no records flagged as synthetic.
  - Sources: [2609.09853](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09853), [2609.11286](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11286), [2609.30055](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.30055v1).
